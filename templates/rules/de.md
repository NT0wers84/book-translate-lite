## Fidelity and format (blocking)

- **Same number of paragraphs**, same order. No sentence skipped, merged or summarised.
- Keep the Markdown exactly as it is: `**bold**`, `*italics*`, `[text]{.smallcaps}`, images `![](…){…}`, `#` headings, `>` quotes, `:::` blocks.
- Translate the text inside bold and small caps, but leave `{…}` attributes, `(…)` links and file names untouched.

## Voice

- Natural, publishable German, not a calque. Past narration → *Präteritum*.
- Choose du/Sie consistently per relationship and keep it across the book.

## Punctuation (German publishing conventions)

- Dialogue in „…“, nested ‚…‘. Comma after the closing quote before the speech tag: „Hallo“, sagte sie.
- Ellipsis as the single character «…». Numbers: 3.000.000 · 1,43 · 20 %.

## Names and terms

- Use the renderings in the Terms table: F (fixed) ones never change.
- Real proper names stay as they are; meaningful names, items, places and spells are translated.
- New recurring term: add it to META.new_entities. To change a P (proposed) term use META.conflicts.
