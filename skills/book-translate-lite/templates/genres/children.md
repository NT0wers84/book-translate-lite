- Simple, vivid {language} for the target age: short sentences where the source has them, no rare words the source does not use.
- The text is often read aloud: keep rhythm, repetitions and refrains.
- Rhymes and wordplay: rebuild them in {language}, even freely; the effect matters more than the literal meaning.
