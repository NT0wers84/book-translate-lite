## Genre: non-fiction (essays, history, biography, popular science, self-help)

- Precision over flourish: keep every claim, figure, date, hedge and qualifier exactly as strong as in the source.
- Terminology: use the established {language} term of the field; keep the original in brackets the first time only if there is no settled term.
- Quotations from other works: use the published {language} translation if you know it with certainty, otherwise translate it; never invent a reference.
- Titles of cited works, notes, bibliography, index entries: keep the original titles and references; translate only descriptive text.
- Numbers, units and data in digits; do not convert units unless the source does.
- Personal names, institutions and places: their usual {language} form (exonyms) when one exists.
- Keep headings, lists and the paragraph structure; do not add explanations.
