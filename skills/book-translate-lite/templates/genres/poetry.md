## Genre: poetry and verse

- One line of the source = one line of the translation; keep stanza breaks and indentation.
- Priority: meaning and images, then rhythm, then rhyme. Rhyme only if it does not force the meaning.
- Keep the poem's register (high, colloquial, archaic) and its punctuation choices, including their absence.
- Titles of poems are translated; epigraphs and quoted lines keep their published {language} version if one exists.
