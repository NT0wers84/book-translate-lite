## Genre: fiction (novels, short stories)

- Voice first: every character must sound like themselves; keep each one's register, tics and level of language.
- Dialogue: the conventions of {language} fiction; speech tags varied as in the source, never added.
- Thoughts and inner monologue: same tense and formatting as the source (often italics).
- Invented names: translate the ones that *mean* something to the reader (nicknames, talking names, places like “the Salt Market”), keep the ones that are just names. Once chosen, never change them.
- Wordplay, songs, rhymes: adapt them so the effect works in {language}; if impossible, keep the meaning.
- Cultural references: keep them; use the official {language} title of books, films and songs only if it is better known.
