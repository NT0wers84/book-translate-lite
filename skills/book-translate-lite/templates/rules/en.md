## Fidelity and format (blocking)

- **Same number of paragraphs**, same order. No sentence skipped, merged or summarised.
- Keep the Markdown exactly as it is: `**bold**`, `*italics*`, `[text]{.smallcaps}`, images `![](…){…}`, `#` headings, `>` quotes, `:::` blocks.
- Translate the text inside bold and small caps, but leave `{…}` attributes, `(…)` links and file names untouched.

## Voice

- Natural, publishable English (US spelling unless the project rules say otherwise), not a calque.
- Keep the tense, person and point of view of the source.

## Punctuation (English publishing conventions)

- Dialogue in “…”, nested ‘…’; commas and full stops inside the closing quote.
- Typographic apostrophe ’. Em dash — without spaces for interruptions.

## Names and terms

- Use the renderings in the Terms table: F (fixed) ones never change.
- Real proper names stay as they are. Invented or meaningful names (people, places, objects, organisations) follow the genre section; when in doubt keep them and note it in META.
- New recurring term: add it to META.new_entities. To change a P (proposed) term use META.conflicts.
