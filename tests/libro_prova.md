# Chapter 1

**[Welcome,]{.smallcaps} Keeper, to the Clockwork Lighthouse.**

**Time remaining: 3 Days.**

Mira climbed the spiral stairs of the Clockwork Lighthouse for the third time that morning. The gears hummed beneath her boots, and somewhere above, the Brass Warden was singing an old sea shanty off key.

"You're late," said the Brass Warden. "The Copper Gull has been waiting since dawn."

*I am never late,* Mira thought. *Everyone else is early.*

![](mappa.png)

She opened the logbook. The Copper Gull had left a feather on the page, and a note written in tiny, careful letters: *Meet me at the Salt Market.*

# Chapter 2

The Salt Market smelled of brine and oranges. Mira found the Copper Gull perched on a stall that sold nothing but left-handed spoons.

"Okay," Mira said. "What's so urgent?"

"The Brass Warden is lying to you," said the Copper Gull. "The lighthouse isn't keeping ships away from the rocks. It's calling them."

> Note from the Keeper's Logbook: the lamp must never be lit on a moonless night.

Mira stared at the bird for a long moment. Then she laughed, because the alternative was screaming, and screaming in the Salt Market attracted the wrong kind of attention.

# Chapter 3

**New Quest: The Silent Lamp.**

That night there was no moon. Mira climbed the stairs one last time, past the humming gears, past the Brass Warden asleep in his alcove, all the way up to the Silent Lamp. She put her hand on the switch and waited for the first ship.

"Don't," whispered the Copper Gull from the window. "Please."

She didn't.
